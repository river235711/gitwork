Our services

Four support lines across the design infrastructure layer — the environment your designers work in, the kit they design with, the decks that sign them off, and the implementation that closes the block.

CAD Support

Design environment enablement end to end — launchers and utilities in SKILL and Tcl, EDA tool qualification and benchmarking, regression harnesses, and Linux CAD deployment, so your designers start work instead of debugging the flow.

Scope
Environment, flows, deployment
Qualification
EDA tools, SPICE models
Languages
Python, Tcl, Perl, SKILL

PDK Support

Foundry PDK enablement and in-house kit development — techfiles, CDF parameters and callbacks, PCELL layout generation, symbols and analog logic cells, plus node-to-node migration and QA across nine process generations.

Scope
Techfile, CDF, PCELL, symbols
Foundries
TSMC, UMC, SMIC, GF, HLMC
Tools
Virtuoso, Custom Compiler, Laker
Discuss this service →

DRC / LVS Deck Support

Rule deck development, porting and qualification — DRC, LVS and PEX runsets with the verification environment around them, validated against QA cells and real designs before they reach your designers.

Scope
DRC, LVS, PEX runsets
Validation
QA cells, silicon correlation
Tools
Calibre, QRC, StarRC, Voltus
Discuss this service →

PD Support

Block-level place-and-route on Innovus — multi-voltage, multi-corner implementation through timing and power closure at advanced nodes. Layout verification, DRC fixes and IR/EM analysis stay inside the engagement.

Scope
Block P&R, MV/MC, low power
Signoff
IR/EM, DRC fixes, RC extraction
Tools
Innovus, Voltus, Calibre, QRC

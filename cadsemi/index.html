Our services

Four support lines across the design infrastructure layer — the environment your designers work in, the kit they design with, the decks that sign them off, and the implementation that closes the block.

CAD Support

Design environment enablement end to end — launchers and utilities in SKILL and Tcl, EDA tool qualification and benchmarking, regression harnesses, and Linux CAD deployment, so your designers start work instead of debugging the flow.

Languages
Python, Tcl, Perl, SKILL
Discuss this service →

PDK Support

PDK development, maintenance and validation on Cadence and Synopsys — techfiles, CDF and callbacks, PCELL generation, physical verification decks, RC extraction flows, P&R techfiles, and advanced package enablement for 3DIC and CoWoS.

Tools
Virtuoso, Custom Compiler, Calibre, SKILL
Discuss this service →

Rule Deck Support

Calibre DRC and 3DStack deck projects run with leading foundries — runset development and qualification, detailed requirement documents carried through with R&D to closure, and tutorial material that trains your team on the flow.

Tools
Calibre DRC & 3DStack, Tcl, Python
Discuss this service →

PD Support

Complete netlist-to-GDS physical design at advanced nodes on multi-voltage, multi-corner and low-power blocks — floorplan, APR, timing closure, IR/EM analysis, layout verification and tape-out, handled end to end rather than handed back.

Tools
Innovus, ICC2, Tcl
